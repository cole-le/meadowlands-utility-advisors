Questions

Before you send a bill.

Is the bill check really free?

Yes. Michael reads your bill and tells you what your building would save. You decide whether to go ahead.

Do I have to replace any equipment?

No. The device locks onto the line you already have. Nothing is torn out, and your utility keeps delivering your water and power as it does today.

What does "guaranteed" mean?

At least 15% off water and sewer and at least 5% off electric. Michael walks you through the guarantee for your building before anything is installed.

How do I pay for it?

Own it outright for about $3,500, or pay from $200 a month for 12 or 24 months. On a big enough bill, the monthly plan costs less than it saves.

What do you need from me?

One recent water or electric bill, as a photo or PDF. For several buildings, one bill from each helps Michael price them together.

Which areas do you serve?

Businesses and property owners across North Jersey, from our office in Secaucus. Call (201) 864-7535 to check your area.
